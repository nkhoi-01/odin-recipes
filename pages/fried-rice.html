Fried Rice

Description

Here's the recipe for the best homemade fried rice

Ingredients

  • Old Cooked Rice
  • Egg
  • Mince Pork
  • Chopped Vegetables (Carrot, Onion)

Steps

  1. Put oil into the frying pan, heat it up and then scramle some egss into frying pan
  2. Put cool cooked rice to pan, stir constantly while on high heat, careful not to poke and smashed the rice grain
  3. Throw the remaining chopped vegetables into the frying pan
  4. Serve and enjoy
