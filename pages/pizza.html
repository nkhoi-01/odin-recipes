Pizza

Description

Here's the best recipe for Italian Pizza

Ingredients

  • Garlic
  • Tomato
  • Cheese
  • Flour

Steps

  1. Finely chop garlic, mix it with cooked tomato and blend them all to make tomato sauce
  2. Mix flour, oil, water, instant yeast to make pizza dough, knead and shape the dough into thin circular shape
  3. Spread tomato sauce onto the pizza dough, then sprinkle cheese on top
  4. Put it all into the oven until cooked
  5. Serve and enjoy
