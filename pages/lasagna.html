Lasagna

Description

Here's the recipe for the best homemade lasagna

Ingredients

  • Cheese
  • Egg
  • Tomato
  • Beef
  • Flour

Steps

  1. Make a lasagna sheet by mixing flour and egg with some water, flattened the flour sheet into a rectangular shape
  2. Make tomato sauce, then mix it with beef
  3. Portion them into equal layers of beefy tomato sauce, lasagna sheet and cheese
  4. Put it into oven
  5. Serve and eat
